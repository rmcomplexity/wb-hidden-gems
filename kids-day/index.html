About the Event

Celebrate Children of All Ages

Come celebrate National Children's Day by going outside and doing something creative! Join us at the Chuck Pavilion at Katherine Fleischer Park in Wells Branch for an afternoon of fun, creativity, and community.

We'll have free popsicles courtesy of Moms and Pops, a beloved local business, plus hands-on creative stations for kids of all ages. Come and meet local businesses and talk to the different Wells Branch non-profit organizations to get to know them better.

This is a free, family-friendly event — bring your kids, your neighbors, and your community spirit!

When:  June 14th, 2026 • 4:00 – 6:00 PM

Cost:  Free — all ages welcome

Introducing

WB Hidden Gems Monthly

Children's Day is also the launch event for something we've been working on for the Wells Branch community: WB Hidden Gems Monthly, our new community magazine. Come meet us, flip through a copy, and tell us what you think!

WB Hidden Gems Monthly is made for this neighborhood, by people who live here. Every issue celebrates the creativity, businesses, and energy that make Wells Branch special with something inside for everyone in the family.

This event is your chance to shape what goes into it. Stop by our booth to share your feedback and let us know what you'd love to see in future issues.

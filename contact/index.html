Share Your Work With Our Community

WB Hidden Gems is more than a magazine — it's a community-built publication. We warmly invite you to submit your creative work for a chance to be featured in an upcoming issue.

Are you a writer, poet, illustrator, photographer, or visual artist? Send us your pieces. We celebrate the talent right here in our neighborhood and love giving local voices a platform to shine.

We also want to hear what you want to see more of. Tell us what topics, stories, or features would make this magazine feel like home to you.

  • Art projects — illustrations, photography, mixed media, and more
  • Writings — short stories, poems, essays, community spotlights
  • Feedback — ideas, suggestions, and anything you'd love to read

Reach out by email at contact@wbhiddengems.com, through our social media, or drop something in the mail to our P.O. Box. We read everything and can't wait to hear from you.

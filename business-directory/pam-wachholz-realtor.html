About Pam Wachholz, Realtor®

An Austinite since 1985, Pam is a dedicated, full-time Realtor® with a passion for serving others and a strong work ethic. Her excellent customer service skills have earned her many kudos throughout the years. She spent 20 years in the insurance industry working closely with real estate agents, title and mortgage companies to assist their mutual clients throughout Central Texas. Seeking a change, she moved toward the nonprofit world as the Community Development Coordinator at the Statesman in 2005 and helped Austin nonprofits get their messages out to Statesman readers. Pam brings this wealth of experience, community knowledge, and genuine care to every real estate transaction — helping families buy, sell, and invest in the Wells Branch area and beyond.

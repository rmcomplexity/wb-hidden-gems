About Maria's Bakery

A family-owned artisan bakery serving freshly baked breads, pastries, and custom cakes since 2018. Maria brings authentic recipes passed down three generations, crafting each item by hand every morning before sunrise so that the first customers always walk in to the smell of something extraordinary.

Whether you're looking for a custom birthday cake, a weekly sourdough subscription, warm empanadas for your office team, or just a coffee and a croissant to start your day — Maria's Bakery is your go-to neighborhood spot.

Located on Wells Port Dr, the bakery has become a true fixture of the Wells Branch community. Maria personally greets regulars, learns names, and often surprises loyal customers with samples of whatever she's experimenting with that week. It's not just a bakery — it's a warm place to belong.

How It Works

🎠 Shared Carousel

Up to 8 businesses in each carousel, each with their own slide linking to their landing page or website. You benefit from collective engagement.

Ads are targeted specifically to Wells Branch and surrounding areas to improve budget and target your actual customers.

Each slide links directly to your custom landing page, your own website, or custom redirect. You control where your customers go.

When neighbors see your business alongside other trusted local shops and services, it creates instant credibility. The carousel format encourages exploration — users naturally swipe to discover what else is available. Your business benefits from the curiosity sparked by the entire collection.

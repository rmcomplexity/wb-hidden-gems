How We Work Together

From Introduction to Impact

A clear, collaborative process designed to grow your business thoughtfully and sustainably within our community.

1

Introduction Meeting

We sit down together to understand your business, your story, and what success looks like for you.

  • Learn about your business journey and goals
  • Understand your vision for community impact
  • Identify your ideal customers in Wells Branch
  • Discuss what hasn't worked in the past
2

Presence Audit

We take a comprehensive look at where you stand today—online and within the local community.

  • Review your Google Business Profile & local listings
  • Assess social media presence & engagement
  • Evaluate industry-specific platforms (Nextdoor, Angi, Houzz, etc.)
  • Analyze community visibility & word-of-mouth presence
3

Growth Plan

Together, we craft a customized roadmap for growing your presence over the coming year.

  • Website & logo creation or refresh
  • Social account overhaul (up to 3 platforms)
  • Content strategy: posts, articles, & storytelling
  • In-person event planning & participation Optional
  • Custom merchandise design & sourcing Optional
  • Customer feedback campaigns Optional
4

Execution & Growth

We put the plan into action with consistent communication and transparent reporting.

  • Weekly check-in meetings to review progress
  • Monthly performance reports with clear metrics
  • Ongoing content creation & community engagement
  • Flexible adjustments based on what's working
